Faq

What's your return Policy?
Our return policy allows customers to return products within 30 days of purchase. To be eligible for a return, the item must be unused and in the same condition that you received it. You will also need to provide a receipt or proof of purchase. Once we receive your return, we will inspect the item and notify you of the status of your refund. If your return is approved, we will process your refund to your original method of payment within a certain amount of days.
How long those shipping takes?
Shipping times can vary depending on the shipping method you choose and your location. Typically, standard shipping takes 5-7 business days, while expedited shipping takes 2-3 business days. However, please note that these are estimated delivery times and may be subject to delays due to factors such as weather conditions or high demand during peak seasons. We recommend checking the tracking information provided with your order for the most up-to-date information on your shipment's status.
Can I change my order after it's been placed?
Once an order has been placed, we are unable to make changes to it. However, if you need to make changes to your order, please contact our customer service team as soon as possible. They will do their best to assist you and may be able to accommodate your request depending on the status of your order. If your order has not yet been processed or shipped, there may be a possibility to make changes. However, if your order has already been processed or shipped, we will not be able to make any changes.
